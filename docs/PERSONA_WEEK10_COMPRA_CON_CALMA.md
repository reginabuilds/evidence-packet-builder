**Situation:** She is about to purchase a household item during a time-limited promotion. She uses her phone, wants a clear answer quickly, and may overlook return costs. This is a test persona, not a real participant.

## Test tasks

## Success criteria

- Finds unresolved return/evidence checks without coaching.
- Understands evidence selection is self-reported, not verification.
- Understands the tool does not recommend a seller or decide whether to buy.
- Can return to the form and edit an answer.
